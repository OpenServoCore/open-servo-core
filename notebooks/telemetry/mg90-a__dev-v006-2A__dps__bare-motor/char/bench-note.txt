PER-TICK CAPTURE ROUTE (decided before the first drive):
"per-tick burst" = the TEL burst (20 kHz, every kernel tick) via `osc sweep --static-load`: goal+arm in one COMMIT,
no seek (no pot), stall permit held by the sweep's lease. Mask 0x1cd (pos, trough, applied duty, current_raw,
vmotor_a, vmotor_b). Chain per cycle: 5.0 V: 6@300 (stalled) -> then:D@300 (spin) -> coast:700 -> coast:700;
7.4 V: D@300 -> coast:700 -> coast:700. Coast = torque on, OpenLoop duty 0, openloop_zero_brake off (both legs off),
i.e. the bridge Hi-Z as torque-off is; chained coasts because a held permit refuses a stream > 750 ms. After the
chain the sweep applies its token brake (82 q15) and torque off. Fallback if the sweep refuses or the capture is
unusable on cycle 1: th drive at 20 ms polls (as the brief says).
E8 (the 960-sample shunt burst) is tried once at setup on the bare motor (osc ident burst --static-load) as a
tooling observation; it is not in the per-cycle loop (time budget).

PRE-REGISTERED CRITERIA (written before any drive)
P0 preamble: meter at rest reads 4.3-4.6 ohm; servo answers at 3M; DPS readback within 0.05 V of set.
   "Nudge" replaced (no pot): cycle-1 pulse current >= 30 counts over the trough. FAIL = < 30 counts -> stop.
Rail guards: pre-cycle vbus_raw >= 1000 at 5.0 V, >= 1676 at 7.4 V, else stop and report.
Q1 rest resistance (rest value = median of the meter's last 10 s of the 60 s settle):
   ONE-SIDED if (median - p10)/p10 <= 1.5% AND >= 15% of stops read > 3% above p10.
   TWO-SIDED if (median - p10)/p10 > 1.5% and (p90 - median) within a factor 2 of (median - p10).
   Neither -> reported as unclassified with the numbers. Split by rail reported too.
   Decay: a "high stop" = first settled second > 1.03 x p10. DECAY if its last-10 s median has fallen by >= half
   of its first-second excess over p10; HOLD otherwise. Verdict = majority of high stops; falsified (no verdict)
   if fewer than 3 high stops.
Q2 no-load current: mean of current_raw - trough over the last 100 ms of the spin pulse, per duty x rail, with sd
   across cycles. FLAT above ~12% if 20/30/40% agree within 2 sigma; FALLING if 40% < 20% by > 2 sigma (then the
   applied duty is reported: limiter if applied < commanded). Breakaway per rail = lowest duty with rotation
   (coast back-EMF on the taps above noise / ripple present). Expected (Aaron, revision): none at 5% either rail.
Q3 Ke = back-EMF / speed. Speed (a) host ripple tach (osc-ident ripple_speed, 6 events/rev) on the last 150 ms of
   the spin pulse; (b) ripple on the tap difference over the first 50 ms of coast. Back-EMF = tap difference at
   coast start. Consistent if (a) and (b) agree within 2 sigma; reported both ways. tau_m = exponential fit of the
   coast back-EMF decay.
Q4 L = R x tau, tau from the per-tick current rise in the first 0.5 ms (10 ticks) of the spin step from rest,
   R = this cycle's rest reading (brief) and, as a second column, the previous cycle's (same seat). Spread across
   stops reported; nothing under 2 sigma is a finding.
Q5 R_rot = (V_on - Ke x omega) / I at steady spin vs the rest low edge (p10), with sigma; a difference under
   2 sigma is not a finding.
Q6 stalled V/I (5.0 V cycles, 6% for 300 ms, before the spin): V from the tap difference (4.028 mV/count, 6k4/1k6),
   I = (current_raw - trough) / 1118 counts/A. Paired with prev_rest_ohm (previous cycle's settle, same seat).
   TRACKS if the ratio median is 1.00 within 2% and no slope vs prev_rest_ohm beyond 2 sigma; else report the offset
   / slope. NOTE before running: 6% = 72 timer ticks, above the 64-tick current floor but BELOW the 93-tick tap floor
   (boards.py dev-v006-2A), so the tap V is expected to read low; V = applied duty x rail is reported alongside.

Two-current slope vs single-point V/I on the bare MG90 motor, 20 seats (2026-10-07). Bench driver: Opus bench-driver agent.
Rig: rev 2A board #1, bare MG90 motor taped to a ceramic plate on J4 (Aaron wiggle-tested the connection stable).
Supply: FNIRSI DPS-150 at 5.0 V only (battery out), ON for the whole run (settles are part of the step), OFF at the end.
Meter: OWON B41T+ in OHMS across the motor terminals, BLE-logged by scratchpad shunt/dmmlog.py -> dmm.csv (one logger,
whole run; only standstill readings mean anything). Firmware: main 2ee13213 per the brief (no sha register on the bus;
fw number from osc status recorded below). Tools: th drive (scratchpad film/tgt/release/th) at 20 ms polls; runner
scratchpad two-current/run.py; analysis scratchpad two-current/analyze.py. Limits 280/280, never raised, no SAVE.

PROCEDURE
- 30 s rest reading before seat 1 (DPS on, torque off): rest_R(seat 1) = median of the meter's last 10 s.
- Per seat k = 1..20: five pairs (6% then 8%), each `th drive <tag> <pct> 0.3 2 20 1000 <csv>` (300 ms OpenLoop, then
  torque off with a 2 s tail at 250 ms polls). All stalled pulses are +duty. Then one 12% spin pulse (300 ms,
  +12 on odd seats, -12 on even seats), 2 s tail. Then settle until 30 s after the spin's torque off;
  rest_R(seat k+1) = median of the meter rows in the last 10 s of that settle. Seat 20's settle gives a 21st rest
  reading (reported, unpaired).
- th guards per pulse: fault, |pos - pos0| > 40 (pot floats ~1985 with no pot), cur - bias > 2300, vbus_raw < 1000.

PRE-REGISTERED (written before the first drive)
P0 gates (fail = stop before or at that point, no patch):
  - osc answers at 3M; DPS readback within 0.05 V of 5.0; vbus_raw >= 1000; torque 0; limits 280/280.
  - "Nudge" (no pot on a bare motor, so replaced as in part 1): seat 1 pulse 1 (6%) current - trough over its last
    200 ms >= 30 counts. FAIL (< 30, the part-2 open-connection signature) -> stop and report.
  - Runner: any th ABORT is logged; a second ABORT of any kind stops the run. Any pulse after the first with
    I < 30 counts at 6% or 8% stops the run (connection lost).
Per pulse: I = mean(current - current_trough) over the on-rows in the last 200 ms of the pulse (t >= first on-row
  + 0.1 s), counts -> A at 1118 counts/A; sd of the same rows; duty = mean duty_applied_q15 / 32767 over those
  rows; vbus V = mean vbus_raw x 3.3/4096 x 5 (dev-v006-2A VSNS 6k4/1k6); V_on = duty x vbus V; taps va/vb/vbias
  means reported (tap floor 93 ticks: 6% = 72 and 8% = 96 ticks; taps reported, not used).
  MOVED = first tail row (torque off, within ~10 ms of off) |va - vb| > 12 counts (0.05 V at 4.028 mV/count),
  or any on-row during the pulse shows |pos - pos0| guard trip. A seat with any moved stalled pulse is marked moved.
  Spin pulse: TURNED if the first tail row |va - vb| > 12 counts; standstill = |va - vb| <= 12 by the next tail row.
Per seat: V6, I6, V8, I8 = means of the five pulses; R_slope = (V8 - V6)/(I8 - I6); se(R_slope) = sd of the five
  per-pair slopes / sqrt(5); R_point6 = V6 / I6; V_b = V6 - I6 x R_slope; implied meter excess = V_b / 0.001 A.
Across seats: spread = sd/mean of R_slope, R_point6, meter rest R.
  SEPARABLE = spread(R_slope) <= 1/3 spread(R_point6) AND <= 1.5%.
  HARDWARE LIMIT = spread(R_slope) >= spread(R_point6).
  Between = INCONCLUSIVE.
  Also: mean R_slope vs the meter's low edge (brief: 4.3-4.6 morning, 4.6-5.2 afternoon; this run's p10 too) and
  vs r_q12 4.71; Pearson r of V_b vs (meter rest - low edge); r_hat_q12 / t_winding_cc movement during stalled
  pulses. Nothing under 2 sigma is a finding; per-seat table, no scalar pass.
